The Roar Grows Louder for the Surging Dragons

KABANKALAN CITY, NEGROS OCCIDENTAL — The athletic arenas and campus grounds of Southland College are electric once more as the Surging Dragons of the School of Business and Accountancy (SBA) return for Day 2 of fierce competition.

Fueled by relentless energy and unwavering departmental pride, the Dragons have continued to turn up the intensity. Every game, every strategic play, and every thunderous cheer from the sidelines serves as a testament to what it means to compete with heart and stand united as one cohesive force.

Embodying their battle cry—“Fall once, rise twice”—the team demonstrates absolute resilience, proving that setbacks only drive them to come back stronger.

As the tournament progresses, the stakes are higher, the energy is stronger, and the Dragons keep surging forward toward glory.

Let’s go, Surging Dragons!
