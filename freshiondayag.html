Beyond the Numbers: BS Accountancy Representatives Shine Bright at the Freshiondayag 2026

Event: September 18, 2026

KABANKALAN CITY, NEGROS OCCIDENTAL — Proving that excellence extends far beyond financial ledgers and academic formulas, the BS Accountancy program took center stage as its outstanding representatives showcased poise, elegance, and unmatched confidence during the Mr. and Ms. Freshiondayag 2026 held on September 18, 2026.

Carrying the banner of the School of Business and Accountancy (SBA) with immense pride, Alexander Dave Suyod delivered a stellar and head-turning presentation on stage. Standing proudly alongside him was Hazel Alvior, who officially captured hearts and secured the prestigious title of Ms. Freshiondayag 2026.

A Showcase of Grace and Determination

​

The campus-wide pageant served as a vibrant platform for new students to display their charisma, talent, and personality. Both Suyod and Alvior embodied the true spirit of a Southlander—balancing grace under pressure with fierce determination. Their remarkable performance on stage highlighted how Accountancy students possess not only sharp analytical minds, but also the dynamic presence required to lead and inspire.

The entire BS Accountancy community and the SBA family expressed their heartfelt congratulations and gratitude to Alexander and Hazel for flying the program's colors high.

​

  • “Thank you for carrying the name of BS Accountancy with such pride, elegance, and determination. You proved that accountants can conquer any stage!”

    • ​

    As the event concluded, the resounding success of the representatives added another milestone to Southland College’s vibrant student life, keeping true to the institution's core ethos: #BeASouthlanderBeEmpowered #DominusFortitudoNostra.
