SBA Claims 1st Runner-Up Overall Finish in SouthlandFest 2026

October 4, 2026

The School of Business and Accountancy (SBA), represented by the Surging Dragons, secured 1st Runner-Up Overall in SouthlandFest 2026 after five days of competition, bringing pride to the department through the dedication, teamwork, and determination of its students.

Throughout the sportsfest, SBA athletes participated in various competitions, giving their best in every game while representing their department with pride. From intense matchups to moments of celebration, the Surging Dragons demonstrated the value of teamwork, sportsmanship, and perseverance.

Beyond the results reflected on the scoreboard, the five-day event became an opportunity for students to build friendships, overcome challenges, and create lasting memories. Every victory celebrated and every setback faced became part of the department's journey throughout the competition.

The achievement also recognized the collective efforts of the SBA officers, coaches, and supporters who helped prepare and guide the team throughout the event. Their time, encouragement, and commitment contributed to the department's overall performance.

Although the Surging Dragons finished as 1st Runner-Up Overall, their journey in SouthlandFest 2026 represented more than a podium finish. It reflected the unity and determination of a community that stood together from the opening competition to the final cheer.

As the sportsfest concludes, the Surging Dragons carry with them the lessons, friendships, and experiences gained throughout the five memorable days.

This is the story of the Surging Dragons—a journey marked by effort, unity, and pride.

​

SBASKOG, SURGING DRAGONS! 🐉💙💜
