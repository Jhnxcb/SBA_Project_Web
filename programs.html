Programs Offered

BSA

Bachelor of Science in Accountancy (BSA)

Focuses on financial accounting, auditing, taxation, and management accounting to prepare students for the CPA Licensure Examination and corporate finance careers.

Key Focus Areas

Financial Reporting

Auditing and Assurance

Taxation

Management Accounting

BSBA

Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (BSBA)

Equips students with strategic management, marketing, and entrepreneurial skills to lead modern businesses and organizations.

Key Focus Areas

Financial Management

Marketing Management

Human Resource Management

Operations

Why Choose SBA Programs?

Industry-Aligned Curriculum

Updated coursework reflecting modern business and financial standards.

Experiential Learning

Hands-on business simulations, case studies, and departmental events (like the Surging Dragons activities).

Expert Faculty

Guidance from seasoned professionals, educators, and industry leaders.
